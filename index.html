about me!

Storytelling with strategy, resulting in visuals that not only look good, but work.

Who I am

  • Creative Director
  • Founder, HER. Design Lab
  • Creative Strategist
  • Visual Storyteller

As a self-taught Creative Director and creative strategist, I bring a unique, intuitive approach to crafting bold campaigns, standout branding solutions, and scroll-stopping digital content. From advertising and rebranding to content creation, my work blends storytelling with strategy—resulting in visuals that not only look good, but work.

I’m passionate about building brands that resonate deeply and drive results. Whether it’s a grassroots campaign or a global brand refresh, I design with purpose, emotion, and intention—always tailored to each client’s vision.

In 2025, I was honoured as a Cannes Young Lions-Kenya winner and selected for the Cannes Lions Creative Academy 30 Under 30, Class of 2025-26. I was also Jury for the Cannes Young Lions-Kenya 2026—a recognition of creativity, leadership, and impact in design.
